# nautical-village-network

A procedural nautical archipelago simulation where you establish trade routes, develop island settlements, and manage maritime commerce across a living ocean.

### Concept & Mechanics

* **Living Archipelago**: Six distinct procedural islands populate the waters, each featuring unique geographical specialties, natural resources, and evolving coastal settlements.
* **Maritime Trade Routes**: Connect coastal villages across the open sea with established trade corridors. Sailboats navigate routes back and forth in real time, delivering cargo and yielding gold and fish upon dock arrival.
* **Village Expansion & Infrastructure**:
  * **Houses**: Increase maximum population headroom and accelerate village growth.
  * **Docks**: Boost coastal fishing harvest and increase the speed of trade boats.
  * **Markets**: Generate gold from every established trade connection.
  * **Temples**: Bless the island to enhance all resource yields and cultural prosperity.
  * **Lighthouses**: Cast sweeping beams of navigational light across the waves to guide mariners and increase trade safety.
* **Dynamic Ocean Canvas**: Animated undulating sea currents, shoreline foam contours, wake trails behind sailing sloops, soaring gulls, and ambient coastal audio synthesis.
